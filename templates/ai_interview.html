💡 Interview Tips

1. Understand your projects
Be ready to explain what you built, why you built it and what technologies you used.

2. Practice technical questions
Review programming, database, data science and role-specific concepts.

3. Explain your thinking
Don't only give the final answer. Explain your approach clearly.

4. Be honest about your skills
If you don't know something, explain how you would learn or solve it.

5. Prepare HR questions
Practice questions such as "Tell me about yourself", strengths, weaknesses and career goals.
